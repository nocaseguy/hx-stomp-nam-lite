# More snapshots (`HX_MORE_SNAPSHOTS`, `tools/features/more_snapshots.py`, `src/m4/more_snapshots.s`)

Six snapshots: a second bank, B (4..6), flipped by pressing the lit snapshot footswitch, and kept in the preset file. Status tags as in [conventions.md](../conventions.md).

- **More snapshots, stage 1** **[verified on unit]** (first MD5 `e9d4f2f800944df1190d2a3915620cfa`, latest `09c73e45264839bf980162b9d1cf1033`, 2026-11-06): pressing the lit snapshot footswitch again flips between bank A (1-3) and bank B (4-6). The redirected tail calls at `0x28dd1640` and `0x28de59d8` swap the three records with a shadow bank in the cave, puts the record being left at another index j, sets current to j, and calls select. So select sees an ordinary change, diffs from the sound being left, and fills an unused bank B record from it. Afterwards j's record and the previous index are put back. Bank B starts as three default records named `SNAPSHOT 4..6`, made on the first flip after each preset load (the parser's start, chained, resets it). A save in bank B flips to bank A for the serializer, then back (below). Next, prev, MIDI CC 69 and HX Edit act on the live bank. Bank B lives in RAM only.
- **Bank marker**: an after-handler on the top view's paint (literal `0x28d96d90`, `0x28dca359`, called with the framebuffer) fills black at (284, 208, 16 x 22) over the stock digit and draws `4..6` with the text renderer `0x28db7aa4` (it draws to the NUL; the length only tells it apart from 0 or 1 character, so unterminated text runs on into whatever follows [verified on unit]) (hx28_boldFont, white, anchor 1 at (291, 203), mode 1: glyph pixels only), every paint while bank B is live and the icon is visible. Its digits' ink, rows 5..25 of the 28-px cell, lands on the stock digit's rows. The flip asks for a paint (`0x28db8508(0, 1)`). Back in bank A, or after a preset load in bank B, the next paint marks the icon's node to draw (`[node+0x24] = 1`) and asks for another paint. The footswitch and parser paths only set that flag, so the widget lock is taken only by the paint.
- **On the unit [verified on unit]**: in Snapshot footswitch mode a press on the lit footswitch flips the bank and the icon shows 4..6; the other footswitches select within bank B; a second press returns to 1..3 with the stock icon; a preset load in bank B shows the stock icon. A snapshot-assigned knob and a block's bypass keep each bank's own value across flips, so an edit is in its record before the next select. A knob not assigned to snapshots is shared by all six, as stock shares it by all three.
- **Footswitch labels** [verified on unit]: in Snapshot mode the Home view's labels (image widgets at `0x2812a05c + 0x460 + 0x24 * i`, anchor 7, set at `0x28dc8a72`) are the same cameras: `Icon_SnapshotNSel` (white circle, black digit) for the current one, `Icon_SnapshotN` for the others, unless the snapshot has a custom name (record `+0x24c`; then a text label). An after-handler on the Home paint (literal `0x28d96e28`, shared with BPM) draws 4..6 over them in bank B: one routine for every camera, which takes the number and style from the widget's image and the place from its position, size and anchor, and on a lit camera draws a white box and the digit in `0x101010` (black is the renderer's transparent).
- **Labels outside Snapshot mode** [verified on unit]: the label widgets keep their last cameras (visible, a camera image) after Footswitch Mode leaves Snapshot, so numbering them in bank B filled black boxes over the stomp and preset names, flickering with every BPM repaint. The Home handler now draws only while footswitch 1's function byte (`0x28128a74`) is 14, as only Snapshot mode sets it; the top icon keeps its bank number in every mode. All-features image MD5 `453c181c210ffbd39f6d57781d170baf`: no boxes in Stomp or Preset mode with bank B live, 4..6 back on returning to Snapshot mode.
- **A save in bank B** [verified on unit]: a save goes through the preset serializer `0x28dea8c4(stream, header)` (called at `0x28df21d8`), twice. It first copies the preset's blocks with their live values into a buffer (`0x28e0256c(preset, 0, 0x2821e61c, 0x16a9)`), then calls the snapshot writer at `0x28dea9fa`; the other serializer, `0x28deacf4` (the other save path and the HX Edit command at `0x28e1c5b2`), calls it at `0x28deaf36`. A load takes the live values as its current snapshot's. Swapping bank A's records in around the writer alone, and flipping to bank A around the writer alone, both saved snapshot 4's values as snapshot 1's: a probe image (MD5 `d829af99…`) counted two writer calls per save, both in bank B, with the flips' selects, and no parse. Now each serializer's entry is chained: before it, a flip to bank A (select recalls snapshot 1, so the blocks' live values are its own); after it, a flip back. Select is let run for these (busy byte `+0x9a9` cleared, then put back), as the load path at `0x28deb88a` does for its own.
- **[verified on unit]** (MD5 `09c73e45…`): a save in bank B is heard as a short recall of the bank A snapshot, stays in bank B, and the preset reloads with snapshots 1..3's own values; bank B starts again on the reload (stage 1 keeps it in RAM only).

- **Stage 2: bank B in the preset file** **[verified on unit]** (MD5 `a9f1be26…`, 2026-11-06: preset 51 saved in bank B with `--writes pass`; another preset and back, and a power-cycle and a new run, brought back snapshots 1 and 4 each with its own values): `0x28df20f2` calls `snaps_file_serialize`, which appends key `0x64` with a bin16 of `"HX6B"`, version 1, 3 spare bytes and bank B's three records (0x728 bytes; the manager's records when bank B is live, else the shadow, else nothing); `0x28df210a` calls `snaps_file_toc`, which then writes the map's count plus one at 0x3d. `0x28deb53c` calls `snaps_file_section`, which finds key `0x64` after key 12's names and stages bank B; the parser hook makes it the shadow bank. Every flip matches the incoming bank's controller and footswitch entries (keyed by their first byte) to the outgoing bank's, so assignments made while a bank waits reach it. **[verified on unit]** The stock firmware loads such a preset as if the key were absent (snapshots 1..3 play), and HX Edit exports and re-imports it without complaint, from the stock firmware or HXtra. **[verified on unit]** An HX Edit export (`.hlx`, JSON rebuilt from the keys HX Edit knows: `data.meta` name, application, build_sha, modifieddate, appversion; `tone` dsp0/dsp1/global/snapshot0..2/...) drops key `0x64`: an import brings back snapshots 1..3 only, and bank B starts anew. **[verified on unit]** An HX Edit full backup (`.hxb`: an `AF6L` header, then zlib streams of JSON: the Global Settings, setlists and every preset as in a `.hlx`) does not keep it either: no `HX6B` in any stream. Bank B lives on the HX Stomp only.
